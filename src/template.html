Good to know

Before you come.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome, but weekends, sundowners and karaoke nights fill up. Call 0115 435 374 or use the booking form to save a table.

Are you open on Mondays?

No, on Mondays we sleep in. We're open Tuesday to Sunday, 8:30am to 9pm.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver in Tigoni and surrounding areas. Call 0115 435 374 to place an order.

Is it good for kids?

Very. There's a kids' menu (fish fingers and chips, hotdog and chips) and a wooden play tower with a slide in the grounds.

Is there live music?

Yes. Weekends feature live music, Saturdays are Nifty Sundowners, and the last Friday of the month is karaoke. Check Instagram for the latest performers.

Are there vegetarian options?

Yes, there are plenty of vegetarian dishes, marked on the menu above. Tell the team about any dietary needs when you order.
